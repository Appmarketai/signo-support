Support

How to get help

For support with Signo, email guy@revoulotionaddiliates.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, the Signo app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support request.

Local learning data

Lesson progress, streaks, saved signs, vocabulary folders, and downloaded text routines are stored on the device. If you delete Signo or clear its local app data, that local information may be removed. There is no Signo account or cloud backup in the current version.

Learning and accessibility

Signo uses synthetic visual sign cues and written formation notes as learning aids. American Sign Language is a complete visual language with regional, community, and individual variation. For deeper learning and cultural context, supplement app practice with qualified Deaf ASL educators and community-based instruction.

Privacy policy

Signo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy describes the current, free version of Signo. It applies to the Signo mobile app and this support website.

Information stored on your device

Signo stores lesson progress, streaks, saved signs, vocabulary folders, and downloaded practice routines locally on your device. This supports learning without requiring a Signo account. Removing Signo or clearing its local app data may remove this information.

Information Signo does not ask you to provide

The current version does not require a Signo account and is not designed to collect your name, contact details, payment information, precise location, microphone recordings, camera input, advertising identifier, or social-media profile.

Network resources and third-party links

Signo may load demonstration media from hosted resources and may open external educator-source links only when you choose to open them. Those hosting providers and third-party websites may process standard connection information, such as an IP address, under their own privacy practices. Signo does not use this information to create learner profiles.

Sharing

Sharing occurs only after you choose the Share action. Signo can create a progress card that may include a completed-lesson count and streak total, then passes it to the destination you select. The privacy practices of that destination apply after you share.

Children

Signo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not provide Signo accounts or a user-submission feature, learning progress remains on the device.

Changes to this policy

If Signo adds accounts, payments, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, cloud sync, advertising, or other data collection, this policy and the app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact guy@revoulotionaddiliates.com.

Terms of use

Signo Terms of Use

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Learning purpose

Signo provides practice prompts, sign-video cues, quizzes, fingerspelling drills, and external learning references for educational use. It is not a replacement for instruction from qualified Deaf ASL educators or community-based learning.

Synthetic visual demonstrations

Some Signo demonstrations are labelled as synthetic AI visual cues. They are learning aids and are not presented as live instruction from a Deaf ASL educator. Source-linked material remains subject to its owner’s terms and copyright.

Current free release

All currently shipped Signo learning features are available without a subscription, free trial, or in-app purchase. If paid features are introduced in a future release, they will be clearly described and offered only through the applicable app-store purchase system.

Acceptable use

Do not use Signo to misrepresent identity, copy protected course material, interfere with the service, or circumvent app controls. The publisher may update the app and these terms when features or legal requirements change.
