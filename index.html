Support

How to get help

For support with Signo, email guy@revoulotionaddiliates.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, the Signo app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support request.

Local learning data

Lesson progress, streaks, saved signs, vocabulary folders, and downloaded text routines are stored on the device. If you delete Signo or clear its local app data, that local information may be removed. The current public release does not activate Apple/Google registration or owner progress reporting. Optional 13+ registration and separately consented summaries are being prepared; neither creates cloud backup. Do not uninstall to resolve a sign-in or launch problem before preserving your learning.

Learning and accessibility

Signo uses synthetic visual sign cues and written formation notes as learning aids. American Sign Language is a complete visual language with regional, community, and individual variation. For deeper learning and cultural context, supplement app practice with qualified Deaf ASL educators and community-based instruction.

Privacy policy

Signo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy describes Signo’s local-first learning and the prepared, default-disabled Apple/Google registration and optional progress-sharing features. Public sign-in and reporting are not active in the current release. The publisher must configure and test these features, align the hosted policy and update store privacy declarations before activation.

Learning progress on your device

Signo stores lesson progress, streaks, saved signs, and downloaded practice materials locally on your device so an unlinked learner can practice without an account. Optional learner profiles store a display name and selected avatar on this device. The first profile keeps existing learning data; additional profiles have separate progress, goals, bookmarks and practice results. Removing Signo or clearing local app data may remove this information.

Local profiles and optional account linking

A local display name can be a nickname. No email, password, profile photograph or contact permission is required. Names, avatars and learning progress are not uploaded just by registering or linking an account. Unlinked learners have no identity verification and can be switched by anyone with access to the unlocked device. When account linking is configured and you explicitly choose Apple or Google sign-in, Signo verifies the provider’s signed identity token on its HTTPS backend before associating that account with your current learner. Account linking does not create cloud backup, cross-device learning synchronization or purchase rights.

13+ accounts and guest learning

The first registration release is for users who affirm that they are 13 or older. Signo does not request a birth date or treat Apple/Google authentication as verified proof of age. Children under 13 and people who do not register can keep using guest learning without account-linked progress collection. This does not replace any additional age, parental-consent or other safeguards required in a particular territory; the publisher must assess those requirements before activating registration there.

Information used by optional sign-in

When you explicitly use an enabled sign-in option, the provider processes authentication under its own privacy practices. Signo sends the signed identity token and one-time challenge to its HTTPS account service. That service records a randomly generated app account ID, provider and provider-specific subject ID, authentication timestamps, registration-policy affirmation and short-lived hashed session/challenge records. It does not store your provider password, email, profile name/photo or raw identity token. It never merges accounts merely because their emails match. Normal launch does not automatically sign you in or contact the provider; opening Account linking checks setup availability.

Optional learning-summary sharing

Registration is not consent to share learning activity. When configured, an unlocked registered learner can separately enable sharing after reading the disclosure. Signo then stores that learner’s nickname, random reporting learner identifier, 13+ affirmation, consent version and timestamp, latest summary and last report receipt time. The summary contains completed starter-lesson count, introduced-sign count, completed-quiz count, quiz question and first-attempt correct totals, current streak and last activity day. Only the authenticated Signo owner can view these summaries to monitor learning activity. Counts are reported by the device and are not verified ASL proficiency. Raw answers, saved vocabulary and folders, full activity history, private draft lessons, purchases, videos and encryption keys are not uploaded by this feature.

Stopping sharing and retention

Sharing is off by default. While sharing is enabled, the latest summary is retained for owner monitoring; it is not a backup or historical event stream. Sign-out locks local learning but does not erase a previously shared summary. In Profile → Settings → Progress sharing, turn off Share my learning summary to disable further uploads on this device and request deletion of the server nickname and summary. If the network request fails, deletion remains pending and the app offers a retry; no server deletion is promised until acknowledged. The service retains only a minimal account-bound, opaque revocation marker to reject delayed uploads. Fresh explicit consent is needed to share again. Account deletion removes these reporting records as well. Backup retention and restoration safeguards require publisher review before activation.

Linked-profile protection and device recovery

On supported native builds, linked learning snapshots are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Encryption keys and account-link metadata are kept in device secure storage; per-learner reporting settings are protected with that learner’s encrypted storage. Local display-name/avatar registry and shared learning media are not encrypted by this feature. Linked learners are locked after sign-out, app backgrounding or session expiry and require verified sign-in to open their saved progress. Unlinked learners remain available separately. There is no server copy of your full learning record or encryption key: reinstalling, losing device keys or clearing app data can prevent recovery of encrypted learning data, even if you can sign in to the same provider again. Shared summary counts cannot restore it.

Account deletion and retention

Account linking includes a Delete Signo account action with explicit confirmation and fresh provider authentication. It removes the account’s provider mappings, sessions, challenges and all associated server progress summaries, not your Apple or Google account. This device’s selected learner progress is retained as an unlinked local learner as disclosed before confirmation. Other protected learners using the same deleted account may need recovery; preserve their device data. An anonymous hash-only deletion receipt lasts up to 24 hours so interrupted deletion can be acknowledged again; it contains no provider subject or app account ID. An already-authorized pending deletion request may be retained temporarily in native secure storage solely for retry, never in browser or ordinary learner storage. Use the original device and retry promptly; no success is claimed until server deletion and local protection removal complete.

What this build does not collect

The current build does not require a Signo account and is not designed to collect payment card details, precise location, microphone recordings, camera input, contacts or advertising identifiers. Names and avatars remain on the device unless the learner explicitly enables the disclosed nickname-and-summary sharing. Guest learning sends no account-linked progress. No progress backup or social sharing occurs automatically. Registration and reporting remain disabled until the publisher explicitly configures the backend and native providers and completes the release safeguards.

External links and sharing

Signo includes its demonstration media in the app. Its account service receives no learning progress unless optional summary sharing has been explicitly enabled. External educator-source links open only when you choose them; those providers may process connection information under their privacy practices. Social sharing occurs only after you choose Share: a progress card can include a lesson count and streak total and passes to the destination you select. This is separate from owner-only summary reporting.

Support website and deletion requests

The public Signo support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it may send standard connection information, including IP address and browser details, to GitHub under its privacy practices; no learner progress is sent just by opening that site. Emails you send for support or deletion contain the information you choose to provide and are used to handle your request. Visit appmarketai.github.io/signo-support/#delete-account for an external deletion-request route that does not require reinstalling the app. Identity must be verified before deleting an account; never send provider passwords, identity tokens or recovery keys by email. No automatic server deletion is promised by visiting the page or sending a request.

Future changes

Payments, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, cloud sync or further data collection require updated policy and App Store/Google Play disclosures before release. Optional sign-in identifiers, nicknames and account-linked activity summaries must be assessed in those declarations; the previously submitted Data Not Collected label must not be reused without a new review. Google Play’s account deletion web resource and Apple provider authorization revocation must be completed before registration is released.

Your data, your choice

Request deletion of your Signo account

Signo ASL: Learn & Practice provides this request route without requiring the app to be installed. The current public release has no active registration; if you used an account-enabled test or future release, these choices apply to your Signo account and its shared summaries.

From the app

Open Profile → Settings → Progress sharing to turn off Share my learning summary. This stops new uploads on that device and requests removal of your server nickname and summary. A pending deletion message means the network request still needs to be retried. For full account deletion, open account management and choose Delete Signo account, then complete the fresh sign-in confirmation.

Without the app

Email guy@revoulotionaddiliates.com with the subject Signo account deletion request. State that you want the Signo account and associated shared data deleted, which provider you used (Apple or Google), and your learner nickname if you remember it. Do not send passwords, identity tokens, payment information or recovery/encryption keys.

Email an account deletion request

Sending an email starts a support request; it does not automatically delete an account. Identity must be verified before account data is erased. If you cannot use the original device, explain that in your request so a safe verification process can be arranged. An unverified email address or nickname alone is not sufficient authority to delete an account.

What deletion removes

Full Signo account deletion removes its provider mappings, active sessions, authentication challenges, server nicknames, progress summaries and consent/revocation records. It does not delete your Apple or Google account. Turning off summary sharing removes only the server nickname and summary and keeps a minimal opaque marker that blocks late uploads.

Local data and limited retention

Signo does not hold a cloud backup of your full local learning or encryption key. Account deletion in the app retains the selected learner's learning locally as disclosed in the confirmation; other protected learners sharing that account may need recovery. Support cannot remotely erase your device data. A hash-only account-deletion receipt may remain for up to 24 hours for interrupted-request recovery, without an account ID or provider subject. Support correspondence and hosting/infrastructure backups have separate retention practices; do not assume deletion from backup copies has occurred merely because a request was sent. Operational retention and verified handling must be completed before public registration activates.

For privacy questions or to ask about a request's status, contact the same support address. Read the Privacy Policy.
